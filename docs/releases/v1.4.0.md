# v1.4.0

The first release of Kairo68, a Sharp X68000 emulator.

- Plays X68000 floppy and hard disk games at full speed on the RG DS, with a CPU clock setting (10, 16 or 25 MHz, 25 by default) and Skip memory check (on by default).
- Games in the 1024x1024 graphics mode (Carat) show their pictures correctly.
- Games that redraw their picture line by line (Carat) and games that draw little run without crackling sound.
- The X68000 mouse works: the touchpad moves it, and Cat's Part-1 moves it with the D-pad and stick.
- A catalog of X68000 games with descriptions and artwork, with every multi-disk set grouped as one game, including compilations that hold several games (Alice no Yakata I + II).
- Every game's D-pad reaches the arrow keys and the joystick by default. Mappings follow a cascade: the X68000 defaults, your all-games mapping, the game's catalog mapping, then your own for the game.
- Scaling anchor: Settings › Graphics pins the picture to any of nine places on the screen, separately for landscape and portrait, and each game's settings can choose its own. Pick it from a 3x3 grid.
- On a two-screen device (RG DS), the keyboard, joystick and touchpad are on the bottom screen, and the D-pad moves between the screens in the library.

Both APK variants and the Play bundle have the same features.
